# ADR-000: Short title naming the decision

## Question Under Consideration

The question being decided, stated as a question, with enough context that a reader who was not in the room understands why it came up now and what constrains the answer.

## Decision

What we are doing, in one or two sentences, stated in the present tense.

## Rationale

Why the chosen option wins given the question above. Name the constraint that decided it. Scores inform the decision, they do not make it.

## Options

Criteria are scored 1 to 5 for this context: 1 is highly unfavorable, 3 is neutral, 5 is highly beneficial. Replace the criteria headings with what actually matters here, for example operational burden, cost at projected scale, team familiarity, blast radius, time to deliver.
